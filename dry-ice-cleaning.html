Dry Ice Cryogenic Cleaning

Our bespoke Dry Ice Cryogenic Cleaning utilizes aerospace-grade solid CO2 micro-pellets accelerated at supersonic speeds. Upon impact at -78.5°C (-109.3°F), the thermal shock sublimates grease, factory undercoating, baked-on road tar, and oxidation instantly from bare aluminum, zinc-plated fasteners, rubber seals, and sensitive electrical wiring without abrasive clearcoat marring or water contamination.

The Cleanroom Execution Process

1

Cleanroom Hoist & Masking

Vehicle elevated on our cleanroom hydraulic lift; delicate sensors, intake filters, and cabin vents sealed.

2

Cryogenic Sublimation Blast

Compressed dry ice micro-pellets freeze contaminants at -78.5°C, creating instant thermal shock lift without surface wear.

3

Crevice & Loom Detailing

Precision angular nozzles detail intricate wiring looms, fastener heads, suspension linkages, and aluminum crevices.

4

OEM Preservative Dressing

Bare metals and vulcanized rubber receive museum-grade moisture-repellent preservative barrier and anti-corrosion lock.
