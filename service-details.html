The 6-Step Cleanroom Execution Process

1

pH-Neutral Foam Wash & Chemical Decontamination

Three-bucket wash method with grit guards, followed by iron fallout remover and synthetic clay treatment to pull embedded brake dust.

2

Ultrasonic Digital Clearcoat Mapping

Measuring paint depth across 48 key body panels using mil-spec ultrasonic gauges to ensure safe, maximum swirl elimination.

3

Multi-Stage Optical Paint Correction

Compounding with micro-abrasives under 96 CRI Scangrip lighting to eliminate swirls, holograms, oxidation, and water etching.

4

Solvent Wipe-Down & Surface Primer

Removing all polishing oils with pure isopropanol prep solution to expose bare, sterilized clearcoat ready for chemical bonding.

5

10H Nanoceramic Dual Basecoat Application

Applying two coats of high-density inorganic SiO2/SiC matrix with cross-hatch strokes, flashing, and leveling with lint-free microfibers.

6

Short-Wave Infrared Heat Curing

Baking each panel at 65°C using IR curing lamps to accelerate crystallization and lock in maximum 10H surface hardness.

Frequently Asked Questions

With proper maintenance washes using pH-neutral shampoo and an annual studio decontamination topcoat, the 10H Obsidian coating lasts 7+ years and is backed by our transferable warranty.
Ceramic coating is an ultra-hard microscopic glass layer designed to prevent swirl marks, bird acid, road salt, and oxidation. For physical impact protection against high-speed highway rock chips, we recommend combining ceramic coating with our Self-Healing Paint Protection Film (PPF).
