Underbody Cleaning

Protect your vehicle subframe and suspension from premature rust and corrosion. We hoist the vehicle to blast away road salt, mud, and road grime from chassis rails, suspension arms, and wheel wells before applying an anti-corrosion barrier.

Starting Investment:

₹2,199

Cleanroom Duration:

2 Hours

Studio Warranty:

Anti-Rust Barrier

What Is Included In This Service

Inverted multi-nozzle 2500 PSI high-pressure chassis wash
Heavy degreaser agitation on suspension arms, differentials, and subframe
Wheel arch liners deep scrub and stone ejection
Exhaust piping and heat shield road grime purge
Corrosion inhibitor spray application over exposed metal joints
Brake line and fuel line visual integrity inspection

The Cleanroom Execution Process

1

Chassis Pressure Blast

Loosening thick caked mud, salt, and road debris.

2

Suspension Degrease

Scrubbing control arms, sway bars, and steering linkages.

3

Underbody Rinse

Flushing frame rails with high-volume filtered water.

4

Anti-Corrosion Seal

Applying penetrating hydrophobic rust inhibitor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Road salt, mud, and moisture accumulate in subframe pockets, causing hidden rust, squeaky bushings, and premature suspension wear.
