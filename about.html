Heritage & Philosophy

The Obsession With Optical Purity

Founded by racing enthusiasts and aerospace chemical engineers, Apex Obsidian was built to eradicate the compromise in conventional automotive detailing.

ISO Class 10,000 Facility

Where Supercar Finishes Are Born

Conventional detailing shops expose wet paint to airborne contaminants, brake dust, and turbulent breezes. At APEX OBSIDIAN, all paint correction and ceramic curing occurs in positive-pressure cleanroom chambers with HEPA filtration, regulated 22.5°C temperature, and 42% relative humidity.

This eliminates solvent entrapment and ensures the silicon dioxide ceramic matrix cures into an impenetrable, crystal-clear 10H solid diamond shield.

Surgical Detailing Standards

The Four Pillars of Optical Precision

Every vehicle entering our studio undergoes a rigorous, aerospace-grade protocol engineered to maximize gloss, preserve clearcoat thickness, and lock in permanent surface protection.

1. Ultrasonic Paint Mapping

Modern OEM paint systems are thinner than a human hair, averaging between 90 and 130 microns. Before any machine polisher touches the surface, our specialists conduct a 48-point digital ultrasonic depth scan across every panel.

This ensures safe, micro-abrasive compounding that eliminates 95%+ of swirl marks while preserving 98% of your factory clearcoat for the vehicle's entire lifetime.

2. 10H Nanoceramic Matrix

Unlike organic waxes that melt under engine heat and wash away with basic detergents, our 10H Obsidian formula consists of high-purity inorganic silicon dioxide (SiO2) and titanium dioxide (TiO2) nanoparticles.

When applied in our climate-regulated cleanroom, the formulation covalently crosslinks directly into the paint substrate, creating a hydrophobic barrier with 118° water contact angles.

3. 96 CRI Optical Inspection

Standard shop fluorescent lighting hides micro-scratches and buffer holograms. Our detailing bays are equipped with full-spectrum 96 CRI Scangrip optical daylight simulation light bars calibrated to 6500K.

Every square inch of paintwork is inspected at acute angles, guaranteeing that the optical reflection under true sunlight is completely swirl-free with a measured gloss rating of 99+ GU.

4. Live Telemetry & Warranties

We believe in total client transparency. Our proprietary customer portal provides live stage tracking, before-and-after microscopic photo telemetry, and digital warranty certificates linked to your VIN.

All 10H ceramic installations include our 7-Year Transferable Studio Warranty, backed by annual gloss maintenance audits and complimentary hydrophobic top-ups.
