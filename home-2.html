Nanoscale Engineering

Why Traditional Wax Fails

Organic carnauba waxes melt at 82°C and wash off in 6 weeks. Our inorganic silicon dioxide (SiO2) glass bonds permanently.

Extreme Hydrophobicity

With an optical contact angle of 118°, water, mud, and road grime cannot adhere to the paint plane. Surface dirt rolls away effortlessly during maintenance washes.

UV & Thermal Barrier

Prevents clearcoat chalking, fading, and oxidation under intense desert heat and UV radiation. Resists surface temperatures up to 850°C.

Chemical & Acid Resistance

Immune to caustic chemicals from pH 2 through pH 13, including bug splatter, bird etching, magnesium chloride road salt, and harsh industrial fallout.

Deep Candy-Gloss Mirror

Adds measurable clearcoat thickness (up to 3.5 microns), enriching color saturation and producing an iridescent showroom reflection.
